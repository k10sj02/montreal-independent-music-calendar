Montréal Independent Music Calendar

A simple, automatically refreshed calendar for less-mainstream concerts, listening sessions, and experimental music around Montréal.

Current sources include Casa del Popolo, La Sala Rossa, La Sotterenea, P’tit Ours, CKUT, Quai des Brumes, Resident Advisor-linked electronic events and raves, Le Système, SAT, Newspeak, Stereo, Datcha, Salon Daomé, Red Room, ESC, Sans Soleil, Fairmount, Cabaret Berlin, and published Suoni, Ursa, and Akousma listings.
